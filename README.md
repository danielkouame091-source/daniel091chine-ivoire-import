# SNDGIR & Transit ERP

**An AI-assisted ERP for customs brokers and accounting firms in Côte d'Ivoire (UEMOA).**
MBA Data Science & AI — Chandigarh University, India.
*Designed & developed by Daniel Kouame.*

> 🇫🇷 Version française : [README.fr.md](README.fr.md)

## What it is
One web application that combines
- **SYSCOHADA accounting** (strict double entry, hash-chained audit log, financial statements),
- **Transit & customs** (files, duty calculator, manifests, demurrage, goods tracking to Abidjan, letter-of-credit release, landed cost, control tower),
- **Machine learning** (vessel-delay forecast, customs-inspection risk pre-assessment, anomaly detection, cash-flow forecasting, explainability),
- **Trust features** (role-based access, TOTP 2FA, backups, personal-data export/erasure).

## Machine-learning approach (short)
| Task | Models | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| Vessel delay | Ridge, gradient boosting vs. average baseline | MAE/RMSE on held-out voyages, AUC for “> 3 days late” |
| Customs inspection risk | logistic regression, gradient boosting vs. “always green” | accuracy **and** macro-F1 **and** red-lane recall |
| Accounting anomalies | Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, Benford, supervised | AUC-ROC / AUC-PR / precision@k |
| Cash-flow forecast | seasonal naïve, boosting, MLP (LSTM/GRU optional with PyTorch) | rolling-origin validation |

**Honesty note:** the logistics models are trained on **simulated** data generated by rules written by the author. They demonstrate the method, not real-world accuracy. Customs selection criteria are confidential; the tool only helps prepare a file. Details: *AI & OCR → Methodology & results* in the app, and [docs/PROJECT_REPORT_EN.md](docs/PROJECT_REPORT_EN.md).
